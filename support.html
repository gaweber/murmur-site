Help & support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to murmur@quantumnet.de. Please include your device and the app version (Settings → About).

Apple Music doesn't play

Murmur needs an Apple Music subscription and access to Media & Apple Music (iPhone Settings → Apps → Murmur). Adding songs to your library also needs Sync Library to be on.

Moving podcasts from Apple Podcasts

In Murmur: Settings → Podcasts → Import from Apple Podcasts. A two-minute Shortcut copies your list over. Murmur also imports and exports OPML files.

Siri and CarPlay

Say “Play <podcast or station> in Murmur” or “Continue listening in Murmur”. In the car, Murmur shows Home, Music, Podcasts and Radio.

Own servers

Settings → Sources → Audiobookshelf, Jellyfin or Plex. Servers on your home network may use http; servers reached over the internet need https.

Explicit content

Settings → Family → Hide explicit content. For children's devices, also use Screen Time restrictions.

Hilfe

Fragen, Probleme oder Ideen? Schreib an murmur@quantumnet.de, mit Gerät und App-Version (Einstellungen → Über Murmur).

Apple Music spielt nicht: Nötig sind ein Apple-Music-Abo und der Zugriff auf «Medien & Apple Music» (Einstellungen → Apps → Murmur).

Podcasts aus Apple Podcasts übernehmen: Einstellungen → Podcasts → Aus Apple Podcasts importieren.

Siri und CarPlay: «Spiele <Podcast oder Sender> in Murmur» oder «Weiterhören in Murmur».

Eigene Server: Einstellungen → Quellen. Im Heimnetz geht http, übers Internet braucht es https.
